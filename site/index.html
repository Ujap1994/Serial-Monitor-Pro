Serial Monitor Pro

The modern serial terminal for makers, service and test engineering.

Instead of a raw stream of characters, Serial Monitor Pro shows structure: a hex dump with ASCII column, decoded protocols, verified frames and measurements as a live graph – ten tools in one window.

Everything for the serial line

Ten tools in one window – from the first byte to the verified telegram.

ASCII · HEX

Monitor

ASCII, HEX, DEC and BIN with timestamps, Δt between lines, hex dump, ANSI colours and a highlighting filter. Send with automatic CRC, header, footer and periodic repetition – with a byte-exact preview.

VT100

Terminal view

A real VT terminal for device consoles such as ESP-IDF, Zephyr, U-Boot and Linux: colours, cursor movement, progress bars – every key goes straight to the device.

.smpcap

Capture & replay

Record every byte of a session with direction and timing, then replay it as a simulated device that answers like the original. Work and test without the hardware.

MODBUS

Protocol decoder

Modbus RTU, Modbus ASCII and NMEA 0183 with checksum verification – live or from a test input, with packet list, details, statistics and CSV export.

AA 55 …

Frame parser

Describe your own telegram – header, length field, payload, CRC, footer – and received data is split and verified continuously.

CRC-16

CRC tools

11 algorithms from CRC-8 to CRC-32C. Byte order, signed position and header/footer framing for sending, plus an independent calculator.

23.5 °C

Live graph

Plots numbers from lines like Temp:23.5, Pressure:1013 – or with your own regular expression per channel – in chosen colours, with statistics and CSV export.

COM × 5

Multi-port

Listen to up to four additional ports in parallel, colour-coded in one view or one panel per port, with byte counters and a bridge between two ports.

TRIGGER

Automation

Sequences of send, wait and expect steps, macro recording, triggers that answer received patterns and a scheduler for timed sends.

SEQ 3

Presets, sequences & send table

Store frequently used commands once – with appended CRC, description, own interval and step sequences – and send them with one click or one after another.

STOP

Stop condition

End a recording automatically on a pattern, after n bytes or lines or after a time – then pause, save the log as CSV or disconnect.

9600?

Baud scanner & auto-reconnect

Finds the baud rate with readable text by itself and reconnects automatically when a device is unplugged or reset.

Plus: light and dark theme · English and German · keyboard shortcuts · line assembly · flow control and DTR/RTS · file sending · import from the former Python version.

No hardware at hand?

Enable Settings → Show simulated test devices. Virtual devices let you try every function without a single cable – and recorded sessions become further simulated ports that answer like the real device.

  • sim:loopback loopback plug
  • sim:sensor temperature, humidity, pressure
  • sim:modbus Modbus RTU slave
  • sim:gps NMEA 0183 GPS receiver
  • sim:frames binary telegrams with CRC-8
  • sim:replay-1 your recorded session

Windows: “Windows protected your PC”

The installer is not signed with a paid code-signing certificate. Click More info → Run anyway.

macOS: “cannot be opened because the developer cannot be verified”

The app is signed ad hoc but not notarised by Apple. Right-click the app → Open → Open, or run once: 

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Serial Monitor Pro.app"
Linux: “Permission denied” when connecting

Your user must be a member of the group that owns the serial ports – dialout on Debian, Ubuntu and Fedora, uucp on Arch. Log out and in again afterwards. 

sudo usermod -aG dialout $USER   # Arch: sudo usermod -aG uucp $USER
